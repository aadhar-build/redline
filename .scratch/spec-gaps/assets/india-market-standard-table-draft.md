"Strength" says what stands behind the figure: **primary** (a government form or statute read in full), **primary, snippet** (a primary source seen only second-hand; verify before approving), **reported deals** (figures that recur in deal reports and broker blogs, not a survey).

Statute rows (C2, C4, L7) form the approved statute list (ticket 29): the model may only select them by id, and Redline shows the row's own text. For a statute row the "Typical term" cell is the exact text Redline shows, word for word; the Source cell holds the URL, the date read, and what was and was not verified (full texts in [statute-texts.md](statute-texts.md)). C2 applies only when the account says the Owner is a Udyam-registered micro or small enterprise.

| C2 | Payment terms, micro or small supplier | Under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act 2006, if the Owner is a "supplier" (a micro or small enterprise that has filed a memorandum under s.8(1)), the buyer has to pay by the date agreed in writing, and that agreed period cannot be more than 45 days from the day of acceptance or deemed acceptance (s.15). With no written agreement on timing, payment is due within 15 days of acceptance or deemed acceptance (ss.2(b), 15). "Acceptance" is the day the goods are delivered or the service is rendered, or, if the buyer objects in writing within 15 days of that, the day the supplier removes the objection; with no written objection in those 15 days, the day of delivery counts as deemed acceptance (s.2(b)). A buyer who pays late owes compound interest with monthly rests at three times the bank rate notified by the Reserve Bank, running from the day after the agreed date or, with no agreed date, from the appointed day, whatever the agreement or any other law says (s.16). | MSMED Act 2006 (Act 27 of 2006) ss.2(b), 2(n), 15, 16.
