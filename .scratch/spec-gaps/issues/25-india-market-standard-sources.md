# What sources could ground Market standard for Indian small-business contracts

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

The owner's market is India, English-language contracts (2026-10-31). Ticket 18's research was US-focused. Facts for ticket 19: public, citable sources on typical terms for Indian SME vendor agreements, commercial leases (leave and licence included) and client/services agreements, plus Indian law that sets floors (Indian Contract Act s.74 penalties, stamp duty and registration for leases, MSMED Act payment terms).

## Answer

Research: [25-india-market-standard-sources.md](../research/25-india-market-standard-sources.md). No Indian equivalent of Common Paper/Bonterms and no measured survey of SME terms. Best primary sources are government: GeM general terms (payment 10 days, liability 1x, LDs 0.5%/week capped 5%) and Maharashtra leave-and-licence format. Lease norms (3-year lock-in, 15% per 3 years, 3–10 months deposit) come from broker reports, not surveys. Law sets floors: MSMED 45-day payment, Contract Act s.27 non-competes void, ss.73–74 penalties, registration of leases over a year.
