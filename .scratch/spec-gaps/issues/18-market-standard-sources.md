# What sources could ground Market standard per contract type

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Facts for OQ6: public, citable sources describing typical terms for small-business vendor agreements, commercial leases and client agreements (bar association guides, government small-business guidance, model contracts), and how each could be cited. ADR 0003 notes the research gave nothing.

## Answer

Research: [18-market-standard-sources.md](../research/18-market-standard-sources.md). Vendor/SaaS: Common Paper and Bonterms standard forms (CC BY 4.0) plus Common Paper's 2026 benchmark of 16,140 agreements. Commercial leases: no free survey of typical terms, only model leases and recommendations. Client agreements: weakest; Common Paper/Bonterms professional services forms. CUAD and LEDGAR record clause presence from large public companies, not small-business values.
