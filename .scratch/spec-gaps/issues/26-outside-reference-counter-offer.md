# What the Counter-offer for an Outside reference says

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

OQ2. Counter-offer wording may only restate or tighten terms already in the document and may not use hedge words (ticket 03). What does the Counter-offer for an Outside reference say: for example, asking that the referenced terms be attached and fixed as at signing? Gates implementation ticket 18.

## Answer

Product owner, 2026-10-31 (asked in session). The Counter-offer for an Outside reference asks that the referenced document be attached as a schedule and that its terms be fixed as at the signing date, changing only by written agreement of both parties. Closes OQ2.
