VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 1 November 2026 between Kesari Cleaning Services Private Limited, a company registered in Pune ("Vendor"), and Tulsi Tiffin House, a partnership firm based in Pune ("Customer"). Both businesses and every name in this document are made up for testing Redline.

1. Services. Vendor will clean Customer's kitchen and dining area at Shop 4, Aundh Road, Pune, every night after closing time. Vendor will supply its own cleaning materials and equipment.

2. Term. This Agreement starts on 1 December 2026 and runs for two years. It renews automatically for further two-year terms unless Customer gives written notice at least ninety days before the end of a term.

3. Fees. Customer will pay Vendor Rs. 18,000 per month, plus GST. Vendor may increase the monthly fee by up to 10% at each renewal.

4. Payment. Vendor will send an invoice on the first day of each month. Customer will pay each invoice within seven days of the invoice date. Customer will pay interest at 2% per month on any amount not paid on time.

5. Early termination. If Customer ends this Agreement before the end of a term, Customer will pay Vendor the monthly fee for every month left in that term.

6. Liability. Vendor's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed one month's fee. Customer will indemnify Vendor against all claims arising from the condition of Customer's premises.

7. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Pune have exclusive jurisdiction.
