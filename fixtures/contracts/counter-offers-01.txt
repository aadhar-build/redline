PEST CONTROL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 5 November 2026 between Lotus Pest Control, a sole proprietorship in Nagpur ("Contractor"), and Amla Bakery, a partnership firm in Nagpur ("Client"). Both businesses and every name in this document are made up for testing Redline.

1. Visits. Contractor will treat Client's bakery once a month, from 1 May 2027.

2. Fee. Client will pay Contractor Rs. 4,000 per month within seven days of each visit.

3. Late fee. Client will pay a fine of Rs. 200 for each day a monthly fee is late.

4. Termination. Contractor may end this Agreement at any time by giving Client three days' notice in writing.

5. Liability. Contractor is not liable for any damage to Client's stock caused by the treatment.
