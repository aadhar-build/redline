LEASE OF SHOP PREMISES

This Lease is made on 3 November 2026 between Ramesh Deshpande, of Kothrud, Pune ("Landlord"), and Chai Kona Foods Private Limited, a company registered in Pune ("Tenant"). Every name in this document is made up for testing Redline.

1. Premises. Landlord lets Shop 2 on the ground floor of Laxmi Complex, Karve Road, Pune to Tenant for use as a tea shop.

2. Term. This Lease runs for three years from 1 December 2026 and then renews automatically for further terms of three years unless Tenant gives written notice at least six months before the end of a term. If Tenant vacates the Premises before the end of a term, Tenant will pay Landlord an exit fee equal to six months' rent.

3. Rent. Tenant will pay Landlord rent of Rs. 65,000 per month, plus GST, by the fifth day of each month. The rent increases by 15% on each anniversary of the start date.

4. Security deposit. Tenant will pay Landlord an interest-free security deposit equal to six months' rent before taking possession.

5. Guarantee. Anil Joshi, the managing director of Tenant, personally guarantees the payment of rent and every other obligation of Tenant under this Lease.

6. Indemnity. Tenant will indemnify Landlord against all claims, losses and costs arising from Tenant's use of the Premises. This indemnity applies whether or not the loss was foreseeable and continues after this Lease ends.

7. Governing law. This Lease is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Pune have exclusive jurisdiction.
