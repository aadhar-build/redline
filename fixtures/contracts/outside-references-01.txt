SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement is made between Brightline Software Private Limited (the "Supplier") and Kaveri Textiles (the "Customer").

1. Services
1.1 The Supplier will provide the services described in each Statement of Work signed by both parties.
1.2 The Customer will use the services in line with the Supplier's acceptable use policy at https://brightline.example.com/aup.
1.3 The number of user seats is set out in the Order Form.
1.4 The service levels in Schedule B apply to the services.

2. Fees
2.1 The Customer will pay the fees set out in Schedule A within thirty days of each invoice.
2.2 Notices about invoices go to accounts@kaveri.example.com.

3. Term
3.1 This Agreement runs for twelve months from the date both parties sign it.
3.2 Either party can end this Agreement by giving the other thirty days' written notice.
