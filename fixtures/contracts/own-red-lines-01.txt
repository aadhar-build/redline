PACKAGING SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 9 November 2026 between Kaveri Retail Private Limited, a company registered in Bengaluru ("Buyer"), and Tara Print and Pack, a partnership firm in Pune ("Supplier"). Both businesses and every name in this document are made up for testing Redline. Clauses 2 to 5 are each seeded to match one Red line written in the Reviewer's own words.

1. Supply. Supplier will print and deliver the cartons and labels that Buyer orders in writing.

2. Payment. Buyer will pay each invoice within ninety days of receiving it.

3. Exclusivity. Supplier will not print packaging for any other grocery retailer during the term of this Agreement. This restriction continues for two years after this Agreement ends.

4. Designs. Every design, artwork file and printing plate Supplier makes under this Agreement belongs to Buyer from the moment it is made.

5. Disputes. Any dispute under this Agreement will be decided only by the courts at Bengaluru.

6. Late delivery. If Supplier delivers an order late, Buyer can deduct 2% of the order value for each day of delay.

7. Notices. Notices under this Agreement are sent by email to the addresses each party gives in writing.
