WINDOW CLEANING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 20 November 2026 between Clearview Facility Services, a sole proprietorship in Mysuru ("Contractor"), and Banyan Tree Dental Clinic, a partnership firm in Mysuru ("Client"). Both businesses and every name in this document are made up for testing Redline.

1. Services. Contractor will clean the inside and outside of every window at Client's clinic once a month.

2. Fee. Client will pay Contractor Rs. 1,500 for each visit within seven days of the visit.

3. Term. This Agreement runs for one year from the date both parties sign it and then ends. It does not renew by itself.

4. Ending early. Either party can end this Agreement by giving the other party fifteen days' written notice after the first three months. No fee is payable for ending it early.

5. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Mysuru have jurisdiction.
