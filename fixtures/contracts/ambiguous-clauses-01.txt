OFFICE FURNITURE SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 12 November 2026 between Teakwood Interiors, a partnership firm in Indore ("Supplier"), and Saffron Ledger Accountants, a partnership firm in Indore ("Buyer"). Both businesses and every name in this document are made up for testing Redline.

1. Goods. Supplier will supply and fit twelve desks and twelve chairs at Buyer's office, as listed in this Agreement.

2. Price. Buyer will pay Supplier Rs. 2,40,000 for the goods and fitting, plus GST.

3. Delivery. Supplier will deliver the goods within ten days of the order or payment.

4. Expenses. Buyer will reimburse Supplier's travel and accommodation costs over Rs. 5,000.

5. Late delivery. For each week the goods are late, Supplier will reduce the price by Rs. 2,000.

6. Warranty. Supplier will repair or replace any item that breaks within one year of fitting, at no charge.

7. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Indore have jurisdiction.
