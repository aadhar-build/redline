HOSTING AGREEMENT

This Hosting Agreement is made between Cloudnest Hosting LLP (the "Provider") and Sharma Clinics (the "Customer").

1. The Provider will host the Customer's booking website on the plan named in the Statement of Work.
2. The Customer will follow the Provider's hosting rules, as amended from time to time.
3. This Agreement runs for twelve months from the date both parties sign it.
4. Either party can end this Agreement by giving the other thirty days' written notice.
